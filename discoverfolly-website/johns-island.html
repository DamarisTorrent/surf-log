Johns Island

South Carolina's Largest Sea Island

Totaling 84 square miles, the largest sea island in South Carolina holds a mix of suburban and rural amenities. Famous for tomatoes, sweet tea vodka and the majestic Angel Oak you're never too far from farmers markets, off shore fishing charters, and an executive airport.

The busy side of the island services the resort world of Kiawah while the rest is about as rural as it gets. Housing options run from marina condominiums, golf communities and equestrian estates with plenty of deep water boating access.

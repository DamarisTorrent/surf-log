Our Team

Brian Kline

Originally from Williamsburg, Virginia, a city also known for its colonial charm and historic preservation, Brian moved to the area to attend the College of Charleston, where he graduated with a B.S. in Corporate Communications. After launching a successful landscaping business he began his real estate career in 2005 and has been a top producer ever since. Brian has been recognized as one of Charleston's top producing agents for the past 15+ years, and was honored to receive the Lifetime Achievement Award through his company, Carolina One Real Estate, back in 2021.

'Raising our family on James Island provides unmatched opportunities to work, play and get our kids to school and back all in the same day. Weekends out in the boat or our beach house at Folly are more like mini vacations all within a five mile radius of home'

Ed Boudolf

A native Charlestonian, Ed began his career in real estate in 1996. With a deep connection to the area, he has consistently been a top producer in sales through varying market conditions. Growing up on the areas rivers, creeks and beaches has given him a deep understanding how proximity to the water shapes the land and creates lasting value. With a background in construction and a 'customer first' attitude, Ed has consistently ranked as a top producer with Carolina One Real Estate for over 25 years.

'Folly Beach and the waters around it were my playground as a kid.. and they still are. I can safely say I've surfed, swam or boated about every stretch of saltwater from the Folly River to Charleston Harbor. Helping clients buy or sell homes with proximity to the water has been a cornerstone of my business.'

Folly Beach

The Edge of America

One of the most vibrant beach towns on the East Coast, Folly's eclectic vibe can be felt throughout the island and is reflected by the people and their homes. Center Street is a lively mix of shops and restaurants and the town hosts several street festivals throughout the year.

The island's seven miles of beachfront and surrounding inlets are a watersports playground for all ages. Blocks extend east and west from the town center and life slows to whatever level of laid back pace one chooses. From lovingly restored 1940's style cottages to modern oceanfront retreats, there's a good slice of everything for beach life.
